About DiffAnyDoc

Compare PDF and text documents side by side. Everything runs on your machine.

Help

Drop two files on the window, or click A and B to choose them. Two files dropped at once fill both sides.

Reading starts as soon as you pick the first file, so the comparison is ready faster. Identical files are detected at once by checksum.

Scanned pages and images are read with built-in OCR. Nothing leaves your machine.

Text: both documents side by side, line by line. Only the changed words are highlighted.

Pages: the rendered pages with boxes on the changed text. Pick the box color with the color button.

X-Ray: both pages laid on top of each other. Removed ink is red, added ink is green.

In Pages and X-Ray, zoom with the zoom buttons or Ctrl + mouse wheel. Hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button, to move a zoomed page.

Each changed line has an arrow next to its number. → copies the left line to the right side, ← copies the right line to the left side.

The Copy menu applies all changes in one direction (A → B or B → A) and downloads the merged document.

Turn on Mark on page, then drag a box on a page and type a note. Use it for changes the tool did not find.

Click × on a mark to remove it. The bin button removes all marks.
