## Quick Commands
- **Run all tests**: `.venv/bin/pytest -v`
- **Run web UI**: `.venv/bin/diffanydoc serve --port 8765`
- **Run native desktop app**: `.venv/bin/diffanydoc desktop`
- **Active branch**: Always stay on `development`.
